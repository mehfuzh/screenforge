Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version

ScreenForge runs on your computer. It contacts a server only when you press Activate or Buy, which send your email address, or when you first make captions, which downloads a speech model and sends nothing about you. There are no analytics, no crash reports and no version pings. Your recordings, transcripts and exports never leave your computer unless you move them yourself.

01What stays on your computer

Everything the app makes: recordings, projects, the audio track, transcripts and captions, exports, and the license key once it has been activated. ScreenForge writes them to the folders you choose and reads them back. None of it is uploaded, synced or copied anywhere by the app.

02When the app talks to a server

Three actions and nothing else:

  • Activate sends the email you typed to the license server so it can return the key issued to that address. The key is then verified on your computer, and the app does not need the server again.
  • Buy sends the email you typed to the license server to open a Stripe checkout page. The purchase itself happens on Stripe's site.
  • Generate transcript, the first time you use a speech model, downloads that model (142 MB or 466 MB) from our GitHub releases, or from Hugging Face if GitHub is unreachable. It is a plain file download: nothing about you or your recording is sent. The file is checked against a known checksum, kept on your computer, and not downloaded again. Transcription itself runs on your computer, and your audio never leaves it.

All three go over HTTPS. Opening the app, recording, editing, captioning with a model you already have, and exporting make no network requests at all.

03What we keep

If you buy a license: the email address you paid with, the plan, and the key issued to it. That record exists so the key can be resent or refunded. We do not keep a profile, a usage history or anything about the recordings you make.

04Who else is involved

  • Stripe processes the payment. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us; see Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Resend delivers the license email. It sees your address and the key in the message it sends; see Resend's privacy policy.
  • Supabase hosts the license server and its database.
  • Google Analytics counts visits to this website: pages viewed, referrer, and approximate location and device, using a cookie in your browser. It runs on the website only, never in the app. Blocking it does not affect anything; see Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts the source, the release downloads and the speech models. The download page asks GitHub's public API which release is newest; that request, like a model download, carries no data about you beyond what any web request does.
  • Hugging Face hosts the original whisper.cpp speech models. The app downloads from it only if our GitHub copy is unreachable, and sends nothing beyond what any web request does; see Hugging Face's privacy policy.

05What we do not do

  • No telemetry, usage analytics or crash reporting in the app. The website uses Google Analytics only, as described above.
  • No phone-home on launch, no install counters, no update pings.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring your data, ever.
  • No account. We hold no record that you exist until you buy a key, and then only the email you used.

06Deleting your data

Email license@screenforge.studio from the address you paid with and the license record is deleted. The key stops working once it is gone, so ask after a refund rather than before.

07Changes and contact

This policy may change; the date above will change with it, and the history is public in the repository. Questions: license@screenforge.studio. ScreenForge is published by Smartloop Inc.
