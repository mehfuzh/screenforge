What it does

Record, edit, export.
One native app.

A recorder and a timeline editor in one window. No Electron, no web view; the compositor runs on the GPU and the UI is drawn natively.

AI editing agent new

Edit by asking

Say what you want and watch the timeline change as the agent works. It reads the transcript, your clicks and the video itself, then cuts, zooms, speeds up, adds music or narration. Auto edit tightens the whole video; one ⌘Z undoes a whole answer.

Zoom

Automatic zooms

Zooms open on your clicks and follow the cursor as you work, easing in and out. Adjust or remove any of them on the timeline.

Camera

You on camera

Overlay, split or full screen, switched along the timeline. The camera follows your face, and Resolve-style colour grading with LUTs makes it look right.

Captions

Captions from your voice

Transcribed on your computer with Whisper, nothing uploaded. Line by line or word by word, burned into the export or saved as SRT and WebVTT.

Voice-over

Narrate after you record

Press R and talk over the video, or type a script and an AI voice reads it: six voices on your computer, eight studio voices online. Each take sits on its own lane.

Any video

Edit any MP4 or MOV

Drop a video on ScreenForge to start a project, or on an open project to add it to the timeline. Screen Studio projects open too.

Privacy and framing

Blur, highlight, crop

Blur passwords and private details or dim everything but the part that matters, timed on the timeline. Crop to the part of the screen you need; zooms and cursor follow.

Native and fast

GPU rendering, native capture

ScreenCaptureKit, Desktop Duplication or PipeWire capture; a wgpu compositor renders the timeline. Export MP4, HEVC, ProRes, GIF or WebM with mixed audio.

Devices and keys

iPhone frames, shortcuts on screen

Record an iPhone or iPad over a cable and show it in a device frame. Shortcuts you press appear as key caps, like ⇧ ⌘ S, so viewers can follow along.

Looks and sound

Backgrounds, frames and music

Set the recording on a wallpaper, your desktop's own, a gradient or an Unsplash photo, with shadow and inset. Put a phone video in a device frame. Pick a track from the built-in music library, lo-fi to cinematic, or drop in your own.
