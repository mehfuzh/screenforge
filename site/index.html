ScreenForge

Screen recordings that look professionally edited.

Record your Mac screen and get a finished video, not raw footage: zooms that follow your clicks and typing, a smooth cursor, clean backgrounds, and a separate audio track you can split, trim and mute. Made for tutorials, product demos and YouTube.

What it does

Record, edit, export.
One native app.

A recorder and a timeline editor in a single window. No Electron, no web view; the compositor runs on the GPU and the UI is drawn natively.

Compatibility

Brings your existing projects

Opens and saves .screenstudio project files, so recordings you already have keep working here.

Zoom

Automatic zooms

Zooms follow clicks and typing, animated with a spring. Adjust or remove any of them on the timeline.

Audio

Independent audio track

Audio lives on its own track. Split, trim, move and mute it separately from the video.

Capture

Native capture

ScreenCaptureKit under the hood. Record a display, a window or an area; click a window to pick it.

Render

GPU rendering and export

A wgpu compositor renders the timeline. Export MP4 (H.264 / HEVC), ProRes, GIF or WebM, with mixed audio.

Appearance

Light and dark

Both themes, dark by default. Follows the system setting or stays where you put it.
