What it does

Record, edit, export.
One native app.

A recorder and a timeline editor in a single window. No Electron, no web view; the compositor runs on the GPU and the UI is drawn natively.

Compatibility

Imports your Screen Studio projects

Import your existing projects (.screenstudio), so recordings you already have keep working here. Compare with Screen Studio

Zoom

Automatic zooms

Zooms open on your clicks and follow the cursor as you work, easing in and out. Adjust or remove any of them on the timeline.

Audio

Split your voice from the video

Your voice lives on its own track. Split, trim, move and mute it separately from the video.

Capture

Native capture

ScreenCaptureKit under the hood. Record a display, a window or an area, with your webcam alongside; click a window to pick it.

Render

GPU rendering and export

A wgpu compositor renders the timeline. Export MP4 (H.264 / HEVC), ProRes, GIF or WebM, with mixed audio.

Captions new

Captions from your voice

Transcribed on your computer with Whisper, nothing uploaded. Line by line or word by word, burned into the export or saved as SRT.
